Care and support tailored to each resident

Every person has different needs. We create an individual care plan that balances safety, independence, and dignity.

Personal & medical care

Assistance with bathing, dressing, mobility, continence care, and 24/7 on-site nursing support.

Memory & dementia support

A calm, secure environment with specialized programs for people living with Alzheimer’s disease or other forms of dementia.

Rehabilitation & wellness

Physiotherapy, exercise classes, and fall-prevention programs to help residents stay as active and independent as possible.

Life at Golden Healthcare

Our centre is designed to feel welcoming and familiar, with spaces for quiet rest, social activities, and family visits.

  • Bright private and shared rooms
  • Shared dining spaces and lounges
  • Outdoor garden and walking paths
  • Cultural and spiritual supports as requested
  • Daily recreation and music programs
  • Celebrations for holidays and special events
  • On-site hairdressing and personal services
  • Flexible visiting hours for families

Support for families and caregivers

Care planning meetings

Regular updates with our care team to review goals, health changes, and any concerns.

Transition guidance

Practical tips and emotional support for moving from home or hospital into long-term care.

Open communication

Clear contact information and a team that returns calls, answers questions, and listens.
